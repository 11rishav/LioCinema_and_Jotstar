#  LioCinema & Jotstar Merger Analysis using SQL

#  Project Overview

This project analyzes the performance, subscriber behavior, and content consumption patterns of **LioCinema** and **Jotstar** using SQL. The objective is to compare both OTT platforms from **January to November 2024** and generate business insights that support the proposed merger between the two companies.

The analysis simulates a real-world business scenario where management aims to evaluate subscriber growth, engagement, content library, watch-time behavior, subscription upgrades and downgrades, and revenue performance. SQL is used to uncover actionable insights that can guide strategic decisions before and after the merger.

---

#  Problem Statement

**Lio**, a leading telecommunications provider in India, is planning a strategic merger with **Jotstar**, one of India's most popular OTT streaming platforms. The merger aims to combine LioCinema's large subscriber base with Jotstar's extensive content library to build India's leading digital entertainment platform.

Before finalizing the merger, the management wants to analyze the performance of both platforms between **January and November 2024**.

The analysis focuses on understanding:

* Subscriber growth trends
* Content library comparison
* User demographics
* Active vs. inactive users
* Watch-time behavior
* Content consumption patterns
* Upgrade and downgrade trends
* Paid subscription distribution
* Revenue performance

The insights generated from this analysis will help management optimize content strategy, improve customer engagement, increase subscriber retention, and maximize revenue after the merger.

#  Business Questions Solved

### 1. Total Users & Growth Trends

* Compared total subscribers of LioCinema and Jotstar.
* Analyzed monthly subscriber growth (January–November 2024).

---

### 2. Content Library Comparison

* Compared the total content available on both platforms.
* Analyzed content distribution by language and content type.

---

### 3. User Demographics

* Analyzed subscriber distribution by:
  * Age Group
  * City Tier
  * Subscription Plan

---

### 4. Active vs. Inactive Users

* Compared active and inactive users.
* Analyzed inactivity across age groups and subscription plans.

---

### 5. Watch Time Analysis

* Compared average watch time between LioCinema and Jotstar.
* Analyzed watch time by city tier and device type.

---

### 6. Inactivity Correlation

* Examined the relationship between inactivity and average watch time.
* Identified whether less engaged users are more likely to become inactive.

---

### 7. Downgrade Trends

* Analyzed subscription downgrade patterns.
* Compared downgrade behavior across both platforms.

---

### 8. Upgrade Patterns

* Identified the most common subscription upgrade paths.
* Compared upgrade trends between LioCinema and Jotstar.

---

### 9. Paid Users Distribution

* Compared Basic, VIP, and Premium subscribers.
* Analyzed paid subscriber distribution across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 cities.

---

### 10. Revenue Analysis

* Estimated subscription revenue generated by both platforms.
* Considered subscriber plans, active duration, upgrades, and downgrades during the analysis period.
